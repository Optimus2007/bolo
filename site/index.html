step 1 · Bolo Sun

Sound becomes words.

A speech model tuned for Hinglish, 142 MB, about 0.2 seconds on an M2. It keeps the English words English: terminal, downloads, folder.

step 2 · Bolo Tiny

Words become meaning.

A 0.8 MB model built from scratch reads what you want and the name you said. It knows “kholo” and “khol do” and “open karo” mean the same thing.

privacy

The cloud asked for your voice. Myna said no.

  • Stays on your laptopYour voice is understood on your computer and deleted right after.
  • Works offlineNo internet, no account, no sign-up. Pull the cable; it still works.
  • Never runs code from speechWhat you say is never pasted into a command. Closing apps or sleeping asks first.
