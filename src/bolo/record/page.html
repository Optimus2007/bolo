Bolo Recorder

Records Hinglish voice commands for testing Bolo. Everything stays on this computer.
Before you start

Your recordings are saved only on this laptop, in the project's recordings/ folder. They are used only to test how well Bolo understands real voices. You can withdraw at any time, which deletes all your recordings.

Tip: press Space to start/stop. Speak naturally, the way you'd talk to your laptop.
Withdraw

Deletes all recordings you made in this session (this browser) from the organiser's laptop.
